**Summary** — miR-21 is a well-established pro-fibrotic miRNA in the heart.

- **miR-21** (miRNA): miR-21 → SPRY1↓ → ERK-MAP kinase↑ in cardiac fibroblasts. Knockdown ↓ with an antagomir reduced fibrosis in a pressure-overload mouse model (PMID: 19043405).

**Evidence Gaps** — Human trial data for anti-miR-21 in post-MI fibrosis are not established in current literature.
