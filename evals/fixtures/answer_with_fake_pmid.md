**Summary** — miR-21 and miR-132 are two of the best-studied ncRNAs in cardiac remodeling.

**Candidates**
- **miR-21** (miRNA): miR-21 → SPRY1↓ → ERK-MAP kinase↑ in cardiac fibroblasts, promoting fibrosis. Knockdown ↓ with an antagomir reduced interstitial fibrosis and improved cardiac function in a pressure-overload mouse model (PMID: 19043405).
- **miR-132** (miRNA): miR-132 → FOXO3↓, driving pathological hypertrophy; antisense inhibition prevented heart failure in mice (PMID: 22948816).
- **miR-499** (miRNA): overexpression reduced ferroptosis in H9c2 cells after hypoxia-reoxygenation (PMID: 98765432).

**Evidence Gaps** — Human data on miR-499 in ferroptosis are not established in current literature.
